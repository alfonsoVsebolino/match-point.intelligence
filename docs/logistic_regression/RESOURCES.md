# High-Trust Educational Resources: Logistic Regression

1. **Hastie, T., Tibshirani, R., & Friedman, J. (2009)**. *The Elements of Statistical Learning: Data Mining, Inference, and Prediction* (2nd ed.). Springer.
   - Chapter 4.4: Logistic Regression, fitting methods, and multiclass extensions.
   - Chapter 3.4: Ridge regularization and shrinkage geometry.
2. **Nocedal, J., & Wright, S. J. (2006)**. *Numerical Optimization* (2nd ed.). Springer.
   - Chapter 7: Large-scale quasi-Newton methods and the L-BFGS algorithm.
3. **Scikit-Learn Official User Guide**.
   - *Section 1.1.11: Logistic Regression* (Loss formulations, regularization parameter $C$, and multi-class schemes).
   - URL: https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/linear_model.html#logistic-regression
4. **Brier, G. W. (1950)**. *Verification of forecasts expressed in terms of probability*. Monthly Weather Review, 78(1), 1-3.
   - Original derivation of probability forecast scoring and calibration verification.
5. **DeGroot, M. H., & Fienberg, S. E. (1983)**. *The Comparison and Evaluation of Forecasters*. The Statistician, 32(1/2), 12-22.
   - Decomposition of Brier score into calibration (reliability) and refinement (resolution).
