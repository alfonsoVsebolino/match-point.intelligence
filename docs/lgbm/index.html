Core Architectural Decisions (ADRs)

Specs: LGBM-001 → LGBM-004
ADR-01 : Validation Scheme LGBM-001

Chronological Expanding-Window CV (Zero Row Shuffling)

Standard K-fold random splitting produces catastrophic future-to-past leakage in tennis ratings. A walk-forward temporal harness was enforced across 4 sequential blocks: Fold 1 (2000–16 train / 2017–18 val) up to Fold 4 (2000–22 train / 2023 val).

ADR-02 : Missingness Policy LGBM-001 & 002

Native NaN Routing Without Imputation or Scaling

Continuous delta features were passed in raw form. Decision tree splitters natively route NaNs (e.g. unknown surface stats or rookie cold-starts) to the child branch that minimizes log-loss. Median/mean imputation was strictly avoided to prevent distortion of delta zero-points.

ADR-03 : Market Independence LGBM-001 & 004

Betting Odds Held Out Strictly as Benchmark Series

Bookmaker odds (odds_diff, implied_prob_diff) were completely stripped from feature matrix X. Trees reflect fundamental sport dynamics. Odds are reserved exclusively to evaluate post-hoc market Brier benchmarks.

ADR-04 : Regularization & Tuning LGBM-003

Constrained Leaf Depth & Early Stopping

A 9-combination grid search over num_leaves ∈ [15, 31, 63] and min_child_samples ∈ [20, 50, 100] identified 63 leaves / 100 min samples as optimal. Coupled with 50-round early stopping, this prevents trees from memorizing sample noise.
